Reprojection self-check for BilliardGame/prompt_first ground truth.

Method: for 6 sampled frames (0,30,60,90,120,149) and all 3 balls, project
the logged Unity world position through the logged camera pose/intrinsics
(pc = w2c @ [x,y,z,1]; u = fx*pc.x/pc.z+cx; v = fy*pc.y/pc.z+cy, w2c =
inverse(c2w)), then independently detect each ball's actual pixel centroid
in the corresponding captured PNG frame via a color-distance blob detector
(white/yellow/red vs. gray table background), and compare.

Result: mean residual 1.72px, max 4.01px, over 17/18 sampled points (one
point -- Ball_1 at frame 90 -- had no detectable blob near the predicted
location, most likely off-camera/near a rail at that instant; not used).
All other points were single-digit pixels, well within the "single-digit
to low-tens of pixels" verification bar. Convention confirmed correct.

Camera confirmed genuinely fixed and overhead: cam_pos = (0, 2.7934, 0)
constant across all 150 frames, c2w rotation constant, matching
TableCameraFixedController (offset=(0,2,0) above table center + rotation
(90,90,0), i.e. straight down) -- a real, already-authored camera mode in
this project (Assets/Vagho/Billiard/Scripts/TableCameraControl/
TableCameraFixedController.cs), not something invented for this capture.
